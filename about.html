My name is Marion and I’m a certified, native French teacher, a graduate of the University of Strasbourg, and an official DELF, TCF and TEF examiner, as well as a DELF and DALF content writer.

After obtaining my master’s degree in French language teaching, I taught in France and abroad (Japan, Malaysia and Hong Kong), as well as online, for more than 12 years.

I have helped students from many different nationalities and backgrounds learn French for various purposes: relocating to France or Canada, finding a job, improving their daily lives in a French-speaking environment, or taking an official exam.

Most of my students continue studying with me after 2, 3 or even 5 years! More than « just » teaching, I believe that teachers can create a unique relationship with their students and help and guide them in many ways. My students regularly recommend me to their family and friends, and if they come to my city, we will of course have a coffee together!

I’m passionate about creating teaching materials and supporting students who are taking an official exam. That’s why I became an official examiner and content writer for the DELF, DALF, TCF and TEF. I know exactly what you need to pass an exam, and I put all my knowledge and experience to use during my lessons.

If you are looking for a patient and supportive teacher and a structured course, you will probably enjoy my lessons!

I’m looking forward to meeting you!
