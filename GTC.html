General Terms and Conditions of Sale

Prices

Marion Lim reserves the right to modify her fees at any time, in which case the student will be notified before the changes take effect.

Payments must be made before the start of the lessons and are non-refundable.

Being late to a course

   Individual Lessons:

The teacher will wait for a maximum of 15 minutes without hearing from the student. If the student does not attend the lesson, the full lesson fee will be due. The lesson cannot be rescheduled.

   Group Lessons:

The teacher will wait for 5 minutes before starting the lesson and will then record the lesson if the student has not yet arrived.

Changing the Date of a Lesson

   Individual Lessons:

The student has the right to change the date of a lesson provided that this is done more than 24 hours before the lesson. In this case, both parties must agree on a new date and time.

   Group Lessons:

It is not possible to change the dates of the lessons. If the student is unable to attend a lesson, the lesson will be recorded.

Cancellation of a Lesson

   Individual Lessons:

Any cancellation must be made as early as possible. If the cancellation is made less than 24 hours before the start of the lesson, the full lesson fee will be due and the lesson cannot be rescheduled. However, excessive cancellations (more than twice a month) are not accepted, and Marion Lim reserves the right not to renew the lessons for the following month.

   Group Lessons:

It is not possible to cancel a lesson. If the student is unable to attend a lesson, the lesson will be recorded.
